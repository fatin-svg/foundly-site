Gentle by design

Three steps. One little reunion.

FOUNDLY makes it easier to take the next step when something goes missing—or when you find something that belongs to someone else.

01

Search with hope

Look through reports using a name, category, or general location. A small detail might lead you in the right direction.

02

Share what you know

Report a lost or found belonging with a clear description. Keep one identifying detail private so ownership can be checked.

03

Reconnect safely

Compare details before arranging a return. Use a staffed public place and involve a trusted adult when appropriate.

Care comes first

Finding things should feel safe.

Lost belongings can be personal. Share only what helps someone recognise an item, and keep sensitive details private.

✓Never publish home addresses, ID numbers, passwords, or private contact details.
✓Ask claimants to privately describe a detail that isn't shown in the report.
✓Choose a staffed public location for returns; don't meet strangers alone.
✓Demo reports stay in this browser. A shared live service needs a secure backend.
